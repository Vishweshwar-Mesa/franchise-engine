Franchise applications arrive as a spreadsheet, and someone reads and matches every row by hand.
This engine reads the file, checks every applicant, and ranks them within their territory.
Every status has a reason. Press Why to see each check, where the rule comes from, and how the score adds up.
One rule never bends: no two stores on one pin code.
When two qualified applicants want the same pin, the tool does not pick. A person makes the final call.
Territories show open slots, free pins and the waitlist.
Settings keeps the rubric editable, with case rules marked apart from our own choices.
The chat answers why questions from the same ledger.
Onboarding starts only after the agreement, GST and legal registrations are ticked.
